Sheet 03 / 05 · Conway rules B3/S23 · 72 by 40 cells

Life

A cell is born with three live neighbours and survives with two or three. Newborn cells show in amber. Draw on the grid with a finger or the mouse to disturb it.

Sheet 04 / 05 · Two pendulums · Ratios from just intonation

Harmonograph

Two swinging pendulums, one for each axis, with friction slowly shrinking the swing. The shape depends only on the ratio between their frequencies, the same ratios that make musical intervals.

Sheet 05 / 05 · Elementary cellular automaton · 256 possible rules

Rule table

Each cell looks at itself and its two neighbours and picks a new colour from this table. Click any result to flip it and watch the rule number change. Each row below is the next moment in time.

Build

One HTML file with no images and no frameworks. Every drawing is canvas, every sound comes from the Web Audio API.

Type

Big Shoulders Display for the drawing titles, Hanken Grotesk for reading, IBM Plex Mono for the instrument labels.

Colour

Prussian blue and paper white, the two inks of a cyanotype, with amber for whatever is new or active.
